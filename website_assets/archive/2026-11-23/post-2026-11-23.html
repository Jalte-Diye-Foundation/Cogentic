The news should tell everyone's story, not just the powerful.

Theme: Women Empowerment

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The maid, the miner, the migrant, their lives are not footnotes, they are the main text.

True dignity begins with dismantling outdated stereotypes and respecting the individual choices, agency, and aspirations of every woman.

How this connects with our mission:
Social education at Jalte Diye Foundation centers on cultivating a culture of equal partnership and mutual dignity across all spheres of life.

Take Action:
Speak up against a casual gender stereotype when you hear one in conversation today.
