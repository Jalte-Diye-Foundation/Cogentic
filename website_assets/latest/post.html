Observing International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction.

Observing International Day of Older Persons reminds us to honor the wisdom, resilience, and lifelong contributions of older persons in our communities.

How this connects with our mission:
At Jalte Diye Foundation, our social education values intergenerational respect and learning from the life experiences of our elders.

Take Action:
Spend time today having a conversation with an older person and listen to their life story.
